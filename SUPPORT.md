# Support — Same spot

**Same spot: Timelapse Camera** helps you photograph the same spot over weeks, months and years, and turns the photos into a timelapse.

## Getting help

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/11maid11/same-spot-privacy/issues/new) and describe what happened, along with your iPhone model and iOS version. You can also use the contact link on the app's App Store page.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are my photos stored?**
Only on your iPhone, inside the app, and in your device backup. The app has no server and no account.

**How do I move my projects to a new iPhone?**
Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or computer). The app's projects are part of that backup.

**I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it.**
Open Settings in the app and tap *Restore purchase*. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase and also works on your other devices.

**The camera or Photos doesn't work.**
Check iPhone Settings → Same spot and allow Camera and Photos.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Give the project a schedule (project settings), and allow notifications in iPhone Settings → Same spot → Notifications.

**Can I import older photos?**
Yes. In the project settings tap *Import from Photos*. The date is read from each photo so it falls into the right place in the sequence.

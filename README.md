# Privacy Policy — Same spot

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app **Same spot: Timelapse Camera** (bundle ID `com.samespot.app`), referred to below as "the app".

## Summary

The app has no server, no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your personal information.

## Information the app stores on your device

Everything you create stays on your iPhone:

- the photos you take or import into a project, and the timelapse videos made from them,
- project names, notes, schedules and alignment data,
- app settings (such as the chosen language).

This data is included in your device backup (iCloud or computer backup) according to your own backup settings. It is removed when you delete a project or uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to take new photos for your projects.
- **Photos (read)** — only when you choose to import older photos into a project. The date of each photo is read so it lands in the right place in the sequence.
- **Photos (add)** — only when you choose to save a photo or a timelapse to your library.
- **Notifications** — local reminders when it is time for a new photo. They are scheduled on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

You can change any of these in the iPhone Settings app at any time.

## Purchases

Same spot Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by **Apple**. The developer never sees your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made. Apple's handling of this data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to export or delete. Your projects are on your device and you control them completely.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app changes how data is handled. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
